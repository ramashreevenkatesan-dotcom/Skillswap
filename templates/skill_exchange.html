How the SkillSwap Matching Engine Works

Our matching engine performs bilateral cross-referencing between your published teaching skills and learning wishlist against all active student peers. A Strong Reciprocal Match is verified when you teach what they want to learn AND they teach what you want to learn. Compatibility scores further factor in matching learning modes (Online/In-Person) and language preferences.
