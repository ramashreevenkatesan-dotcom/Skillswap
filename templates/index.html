Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about getting started on SkillSwap.

What is a "Reciprocal Skill Exchange"?

A reciprocal skill exchange occurs when two students have complementary skills: Student A teaches a skill Student B desires (e.g., Python Backend), and Student B teaches a skill Student A desires (e.g., Figma UI Design). Both exchange knowledge equally!

Can I request a skill even if I don't have a reciprocal match yet?

Yes! You can send a direct "Request to Learn" for any active skill listing. The teacher will review your learning goals and can accept your session request directly.

How are learning sessions conducted?

Once an exchange or learning request is accepted, both participants can arrange sessions using the built-in scheduler, complete with video call links (e.g., Google Meet / Zoom) or campus meetups.

How does peer rating and feedback work?

To ensure genuine quality and eliminate fake ratings, reviews are only unlocked once both participants mark an exchange as Completed. Ratings reflect real student peer experiences.
